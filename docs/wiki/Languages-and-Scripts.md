# Languages and scripts

**App settings → Language and script.**

- **Languages:** English, Italian, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese,
  Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Arabic (right-to-left). *Automatic* follows the
  system language when Chronexus has it, otherwise English.
- **Scripts** — decorative renderings of the interface labels: Italian Braille,
  International Morse, Minecraft Standard Galactic, Espruar and Dethek. Text fields and
  screen readers keep ordinary text.
- The change is immediate and remembered. **English · Reset language and script** stays
  readable whatever you chose.

Your notes, pages, citations and backups are **never translated** — only the interface.
Translations other than English were prepared with AI help and still await review by
native speakers; please report awkward wording.

A **transcription laboratory** lets you try the scripts on any text without changing your
campaign.

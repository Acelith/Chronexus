# Character sheet

Each campaign has **one** playable character: yours.

## The sheet

Choose **Character → Create character**. The sheet is generic: fill in only what your game
uses — identity, rules system, level and experience, hit points (current, maximum,
temporary), conditions, notes, abilities, skills, saving throws, defences and movement.

For **D&D 5e (2014)** there is a manual preset following the four-page official sheet:
combat and senses, identity and proficiencies, background and appearance, weapons and
ammunition, money and load, spellcasting. **Add D&D fields (2014)** inserts the missing
fields without touching your values; **Add slots 1st–9th** creates spell slots at zero.

> Chronexus **does not calculate** modifiers, proficiency, slots or rests. Every number is
> yours to enter; nothing is consumed automatically.

The sheet uses explicit **Save character** / **Save entry** buttons; quick +/− buttons on
resources and the equipped/prepared toggles save immediately.

## Resources and slots

Personal resources (rage, ki, superiority dice…) and spell slots have a maximum you
choose and +/− buttons. Every change is saved.

## Inventory and item origin

**Inventory → Add item**: name, quantity (zero allowed), category, weight, value,
equipped, consumable, description and notes. An item can remember **where it came from**:
the session and the exact paragraph, the character who gave it, the place and the quest.
*"Who gave me this key?"* — open the item and jump to the moment.

If the original paragraph is later deleted, the origin is marked as missing and the
whole session can still be opened. Origins never create relationships in the graph.

## Abilities and spells

Write your own abilities and spells with system, rank, school, activation, duration,
range, components and separate *known* / *prepared* markers. To start from official
text, use the [rules wiki or the SRD catalog](Rules-Wiki-and-SRD.md): an imported spell is
an editable copy that keeps its source, edition and licence visible.

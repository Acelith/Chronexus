# Dice roller

Open **Dice roller** from the campaign menu.

- Pick any mix of d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20 and d100 — up to 50 of one type and 100 dice in
  one roll — and roll.
- The dragon circle animates the roll and reveals the dice; you can skip the animation,
  and it respects your system's *reduce motion* setting. The scene shows up to 14 dice;
  the detail always lists every value.
- Each campaign keeps its own **roll history**, included in backups.

The roller never applies a result to your sheet.

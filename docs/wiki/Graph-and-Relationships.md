# Graph and relationships

The **Graph** shows your pages and sessions as a map of connections. It draws two very
different kinds of line:

| Line | Meaning | Who creates it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dashed | **Mention** — the page is named in that note | Chronexus, from your `[[` links |
| Solid, with an arrow and a label | **Relationship** — something you believe is true | You, explicitly |

## Declaring a relationship

A relationship goes *from* one page *to* another and has:

- a **label** ("serves", "is the sister of", "owes money to");
- a **certainty**: confirmed, suspected, disproven or unknown;
- an optional **source** — the session, page or exact paragraph where you learnt it — plus
  evidence and notes.

Press **Save relationship** to store it. While a relationship form is open, Chronexus
does not let you navigate away by accident. Deleting a relationship asks for confirmation
and never touches pages, notes or mentions.

## Exploring

- The graph opens on a readable map of up to 18 nodes. **Show all nodes** and
  **Layout → Organic** reveal more.
- Filters: node type, mentions or relationships, certainty, title/alias, source session,
  and "neighbours within 1–2 steps" of a selected page.
- Click a node to highlight its connections and open it; click a line to see its source
  and evidence.
- A view shows at most 150 nodes and 400 lines; when filters need narrowing, Chronexus
  tells you how many were left out.
- An **accessible list** shows the same content for keyboard and screen-reader use.

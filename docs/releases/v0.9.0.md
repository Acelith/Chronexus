# Chronexus 0.9.0 — Shared campaigns that keep themselves in sync

This release adds the optional Chronexus Server companion and everything the app needs to use
it. Without a server, nothing changes: Chronexus stays fully offline, and the sharing screens
contact nothing until you press a button or switch automatic synchronization on for a campaign.
It also ships the English-first work of 0.8.1, which was never released on its own.

**Choose what leaves your device, item by item.** Pages, sessions, relationships and party
members can stay only on this computer, be kept as a **private copy** that your own other
computers can download, or be **shared** with the other players. Selected sections of the
character sheet (never your notes) and individual images can be shared read-only. A summary
always says how many items are in each state. What other players share stays attributed to them
and never becomes a fact of your campaign.

**Automatic synchronization, opt-in per campaign.** Changes are saved on this device first and
sent a moment later; editing never waits for the network. The header badge and the Sharing view
say whether a change is only on this device, being sent, saved on the server, waiting because the
server is not reachable, in conflict, or stopped. If the server is down or the app crashes
mid-send, the change is retried safely and applied once. Two computers of the same account stay
aligned, concurrent edits become a conflict that shows both versions, and nothing is overwritten
silently. Download a campaign you keep on the server onto a second computer from App settings.

**Revocation and deletion are yours to choose.** If you are removed from a campaign or it is
deleted, the copies received from others are cleared, your own items stay, and synchronization
stops with a clear reason. You can delete your copy on the server, delete a campaign you own
(by typing its name) or delete your account; none of them touches what is on your computer.

**English-first (0.8.1).** English is the default, source and fallback language for new
profiles; Italian and the other languages stay selectable and existing choices are preserved.

Schema 021 (additive; sharing tables were rebuilt with their data copied). Back up before
upgrading: older builds do not read a newer archive.

**Verified (Windows x64):** 168 Rust tests (4 more run against a real Chronexus Server build),
179 frontend tests, Clippy, ESLint, TypeScript and the translation check; the sharing controls
in the real application window with Axe reporting no violations; two computers replicating
through a real server with a median of about 160 ms from typing to the other computer, a server
restart recovered automatically, conflicts, revocation, deletion, and shared images and sheet
sections. **Not verified:** macOS and Linux, ARM64, installation/upgrade of the installer,
sign-in and download flows in the window (they need an HTTPS server), real networks and TLS.
The server has its own release; this installer works with servers that support the
incremental-sync capability.
